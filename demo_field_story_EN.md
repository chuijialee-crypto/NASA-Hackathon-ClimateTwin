# Demo Field Story

**For the pitch, demo video, and Space Apps project page.**
Demo field: Kedah padi field, Malaysia — lat 6.12, lon 100.37

---

## One-line framing

**Kedah is Malaysia's rice bowl. This is a padi field that feeds the country — and its climate is already shifting.**

---

## The farmer's question (demo opening)

Picture a rice farmer in Kedah, in Malaysia's northwest. Their family has grown padi on the same field for generations. But the monsoon arrives less predictably each year, and hot days are becoming more common. Their question is not "what should I plant next season?" It is "how much longer can this field grow rice at all?"

Climate Twin answers: your field is becoming a hotter place that exists today — and farmers there have already adapted.

---

## Why this field (facts, all with sources)

- **Kedah is the traditional rice bowl of Malaysia** — described as such by the World Bank.
  Source: World Bank, Muda River Irrigation Project — https://documents.worldbank.org/en/publication/documents-reports/documentdetail/746071468049814871

- **The MADA / Muda Irrigation Scheme in Kedah is the largest rice granary in Malaysia**, covering roughly 100,000 ha of paddy (about 97,000 ha under double cropping).
  Source: Muda Agricultural Development Authority data, cited in multiple studies — e.g. https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s41403-022-00382-8

- **Rice farmers in Kedah are already affected by climate change**; rising temperature is linked to lower rice yield in Malaysia.
  Sources: "Impact of Climate Change on Rice Yield in Malaysia" (MDPI, 2021) — https://www.mdpi.com/2077-0472/11/6/569 ; study on Kedah rice-farmer poverty under a changing climate — https://www.researchgate.net/publication/351640586

## Safe phrasing rules (read before you present)

- The farmer is a **representative example**, not a specific named person. Say "a typical Kedah rice farmer" — do not invent a real-sounding name.
- "Hotter" is well supported. Only say the field is getting **"drier"** if the engine's trend for this field actually shows declining rainfall — confirm against Role 1's output before the demo.
- Prefer "one of Malaysia's largest rice-producing regions" or the sourced "largest rice granary" phrasing. Avoid quoting an exact national percentage unless verified against an official source.

---

## Caveats (keep these visible / say them before judges ask)

1. NASA POWER is gridded climate data at roughly 50 km, not a measurement of this specific field.
2. We do not forecast weather, yield, or profit.
3. A linear trend is a simplifying assumption, not a climate projection.
4. Confirm any planting decision with a local agricultural adviser.
